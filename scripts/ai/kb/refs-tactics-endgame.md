# Reference index — tactics, attack, defence, calculation, endgames

@ vukovic-art-of-attack | Vladimir Vuković | The Art of Attack in Chess | 1965 | attack,tactics |
- The classic bishop sacrifice on h7 (Greek gift): works when a knight can reach g5, the queen can join on h5, and the defender cannot cover h7/f6 or escape with the king.
- Attack on the castled king: the attacker needs superior force near the king; pawn storms against opposite-side castling.
- Mating patterns: the attack on the back rank, on the h-file, and the combined attack of queen and minor pieces.
- The attack must be prepared: open lines towards the king and bring pieces closer before striking.

@ spielmann-sacrifice | Rudolf Spielmann | The Art of Sacrifice in Chess | 1935 | attack,tactics |
- Classification: sham sacrifices (material regained by force) and real sacrifices (long-term compensation that cannot be calculated to the end).
- Real sacrifices for development, for the attack on the king, or to keep the enemy king in the centre.

@ renaud-kahn-checkmate | Georges Renaud & Victor Kahn | The Art of the Checkmate | 1953 | tactics,attack |
- Named mating patterns: Anastasia's mate, Boden's mate, Arabian mate, Greco's mate, Damiano's mate, Legall's mate, the smothered mate, the epaulette mate.
- Recognising the pattern makes the sacrifice that leads to it visible.
